# XPR Network Development Fund

## Overview

The **XPR Grants Program** is funded by the **XPR Grants account (`xprgrants`)** from the **XPR Network Development Fund**.

The purpose of this program is to empower developers, builders, and small businesses to create tools, dApps, and infrastructure that strengthen the XPR Network ecosystem.

All grant proposals **must be submitted directly through the [XPR Governance Dashboard](https://gov.xprnetwork.org)**.  
This is the **only** accepted method of application — GitHub submissions are no longer used.

Once submitted through the dashboard, your proposal will go through the following process:

1. **DAO Dash Community Vote** – The community reviews and votes on your proposal.  
2. **XPR Consortium Approval** – Based on DAO Dash results and milestone verification, the Consortium finalizes funding.



---

## Grant Guidelines

Both **individuals** and **companies** are eligible to apply for funding.  
The focus is on supporting useful, secure, and sustainable projects — with preference given to **open-source initiatives**.

|                       | **Individual** | **Small Team** | **Company** |
|-----------------------|----------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Standard Grant** | Up to $1,000 | Up to $2,500 | Up to $5,000 |
| **Open Source Grant** | Up to $2,500 | Up to $10,000 | Up to $25,000 |

### Requirements

#### Individual
- Must have an **XPR Network account**  
- Account must have **KYC verification**  
- Must demonstrate **proven technical or development experience**

#### Small Team
- Must have an **XPR Network account**  
- Account must have **KYC verification**  
- Must have a **live project** or demonstrate team experience  
- Must complete a **live call** with a Consortium member

#### Company
- Must have an **XPR Network account**  
- Account must have **KYC verification**  
- Must have a **live product** or active project  
- Must complete an **in-person (if feasible)** or **virtual meeting** with Metallicus or the Consortium

> **Special Company Grants** may be considered for essential infrastructure or critical community services.

All milestones and deliverables are reviewed and monitored by the **XPR Consortium** and **Core Developers**.

## Application Process

All grant applications are now managed **exclusively** through the [**XPR Governance Dashboard**](https://gov.xprnetwork.org).

When submitting your proposal:
1. Go to [gov.xprnetwork.org](https://gov.xprnetwork.org).  
2. Create a **new proposal** under the Grants category.  
3. Clearly specify:
   - **Funding amount** requested (in USD / XPR)
   - **Number of milestones**
   - **Detailed project description**
   - **Team information and experience**
   - **Links to repositories, prototypes, or demos**

Be transparent and clear with the community — this directly affects how token holders vote on your proposal.

---

## Review & Approval

After submission:

1. The **XPR Consortium** reviews your proposal to ensure it meets the minimum criteria.  
2. If approved for community voting, it will be **published to [DAO Dash](https://gov.xprnetwork.org)** for on-chain voting.  
3. If the proposal **passes the community vote**, the **Consortium will initiate an on-chain multisig (msig)** for the first milestone payment.  

If the Consortium requests clarification, applicants must respond within **2 weeks**, or the proposal will be closed.

---

## Milestone Process

- Projects must **report progress and milestones** through updates linked in the governance dashboard.  
- Milestones are verified by the **XPR Consortium** before each payment.  
- Failure to provide updates may delay or cancel future milestone payments.

---

## Payments

- Payments are made either:
  - Directly in **XPR** from the **XPR Grants account (`xprgrants`)**, **or**
  - In **XMD or USDC stablecoin**, sent by the **core developers** after conversion from XPR to stablecoin.

- Each payout is executed through an **on-chain multisig (msig)** approved by **the XPR Network Consortium members**.  
- First-time grant recipients will receive payment **per milestone**.

---

## Project Expectations

Applicants should demonstrate:
- A clear understanding of **XPR Network or Antelope technology**  
- Evidence of **prior work, code, or prototypes**  
- Commitment to **maintaining** the project post-deployment  
- Transparent communication and **community engagement**

Strong preference is given to open-source, auditable, and community-beneficial projects.
